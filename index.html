What is tank beer — and why is it better?

"Tankové pivo" (tank beer) is unpasteurised lager delivered fresh from the brewery into large copper tanks behind the bar. No keg, no extra CO₂, no long shelf life. Here's why people go out of their way for it.

🛢️

Tank, not barrel

Instead of steel kegs, the beer rests in large copper or foil-lined tanks. The beer is pushed out by gently compressed air around a collapsible bag, so no extra carbon dioxide is forced into the beer. The result is a softer, rounder, less gassy pour.

🌾

Unpasteurised & fresh

Tankbier is not pasteurised. Pasteurisation heats beer to make it shelf-stable but flattens the delicate hop and malt aromas. Tank beer skips that step, so the flavour is fuller and tastes closer to drinking it at the brewery.

🚚

From Plzeň in 48 hours

Because it isn't pasteurised, it has to be drunk fast. Tankbier is delivered from the Pilsner Urquell brewery and kept cold, typically reaching the bar within about 48 hours and served within days — peak freshness.

🍺

The proper pour

Tankbier is made for the traditional Czech "hladinka" pour — a thick, creamy, wet foam head poured in one smooth motion. The foam protects the beer and keeps it smooth from the first sip to the last.

Tank beer 🛢️

  • Unpasteurised — full aroma
  • No added CO₂ — soft & smooth
  • Days fresh from the brewery
  • Made for the creamy Czech pour

Standard keg / barrel

  • Usually pasteurised for shelf life
  • Pushed with added CO₂ — gassier
  • Can sit for weeks or months
  • Often a thinner, fizzier head
